What is Aviator?

Aviator is an adrenaline-fueled crash game. The principle is pretty simple: bet on the direction the line graph of the multiplier will go and cash out before the graph crashes. The multiplier goes up as much as 100X+ for a huge reward for those timing their exit correctly.

🎲

Provably Fair Algorithm

The core of Aviator's appeal lies in its "Provably Fair" algorithm, which ensures that every game outcome is genuinely random and fair. This system allows players to verify the fairness of each game round. The algorithm generates a hash before each round, which is then used to determine the crash point.

💰

Betting Range

The betting range in Aviator is inclusive, with minimum stakes as low as a few cents and maximum stakes up to thousands. This makes it attractive to both the casual player and the high-stake gambler. The game is hosted on a platform that is rather easy to navigate.

How to Play Aviator?

1

Place your bet

Start by selecting your stake within the given betting range before the round begins.

2

Monitor the graph

Once the game begins, a line graph starts to ascend. The multiplier increases as the line goes up.

3

Cash out

Click the cash-out button before the line crashes. The multiplier at the point you cash out determines your winnings.

Tips and Tricks to Win

  • Start with smaller bets. Especially if you're new to the game, begin with smaller bets to get a feel for the game's pace and rhythm.
  • Set a cash-out strategy. Decide in advance at what multiplier you will cash out. Consistency can help in managing risks and maximizing profits.
  • Watch for patterns. While the game is random, some players believe that observing the history of past rounds can help predict when a crash might occur. Use this information cautiously.
  • Use auto cashout. Set a certain multiplier at which the game automatically cashes out for you, saving you from greed.
  • Keep emotions in check. It is easy to get caught up in the excitement and make impulsive decisions. Stay calm and keep to your strategy.
  • Take breaks. If you are in a losing streak, it might be wise to step away. Sometimes a fresh look helps in bringing back better judgment.
